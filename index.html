Mercado Ventures is the commercial platform for a portfolio of entrepreneurial activities spanning the practical and the technical. Its work is grounded in operating businesses, building products and navigating the realities of delivering specialist services—not simply identifying opportunities from a distance.

Across boat design, marine electrical work, vessel registration and connected technology, our approach is hands-on: understand the need, bring the right people and tools together, then make the solution work.

From Singapore, we work with partners and customers across borders. We weigh each opportunity against the skills, time and resources it will take to deliver. Our longer-term interests also include property and other investments.
